# Explicit Assumptions and Boundary Limitations

In accordance with Section 72 of the Master Specification, the system operates on the following documented assumptions and boundaries:

1. **GFR Organization Applicability:** General Financial Rules apply to Central Government Ministries, Departments, and attached/subordinate autonomous bodies. State institutions or private entities may adopt modified procurement policies.
2. **Approval Authority Delegation:** The system strictly separates procurement procedural routes from approval authorities. Approval roles (e.g. Dean, HoD, Director) are only returned when an explicit institutional delegation record is loaded; otherwise, the system returns "Competent Authority — institutional delegation must be verified".
3. **GeM First Mandate:** In compliance with GFR Rule 149, non-GeM procurement routes (Rule 154, Rule 155, Limited Tender) are only permissible when non-availability on GeM has been documented via a verified Non-Availability Certificate (NAC) or verified search.
4. **Special Research Provisions:** Higher thresholds under Rule 154 (up to ₹2 Lakh) and Rule 155 (up to ₹25 Lakh) are not triggered merely by research keywords. The institution must be verified as eligible under the DoE OM and the procurement must be for scientific research apparatus or consumables.
5. **Synthetic / Demonstration Policy:** Internal institutional policies (such as Institute Manual v3.1) are clearly flagged as demonstration overlays and never presented as official statutory central government policy.
6. **Split Purchases:** Demands divided across proximate dates (default 30 days) for similar items trigger a neutral flag under GFR Rule 144.
7. **Decision Support Nature:** Procurement Compass is a decision-support advisory system, not a financial sanctioning authority. It does not replace the statutory competent authority.
