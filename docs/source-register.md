# Authoritative Source Register

Procurement Compass enforces an explicit **Source Authority Hierarchy** (§6):
- **Level 1 — Primary Government Sources** (DoE, MoF, GFR text, OMs, Official Manuals, GeM GTC)
- **Level 2 — Institutional Primary Sources** (Approved Purchase Manuals, Delegation Matrices)
- **Level 3 — Official Forms & Templates** (Rule 154 Certificate, Rule 155 Committee Format, PAC Form)
- **Level 4 — Secondary Sources** (Training materials, guidelines)
- **Level 5 — Unverified**
